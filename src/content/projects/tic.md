Tic keeps your task lists on the desktop as floating sticky notes, instead of behind a menu bar click. Each note is its own checklist: glance at it, check things off, drag it around. It's SwiftUI hosted in AppKit panels, backed by SQLite through GRDB.

## Highlights

- Borderless, draggable notes that can float on top or show on every Space
- Subtasks up to three levels, multiline tasks and inline Markdown
- Solid colour or Liquid Glass notes, and roll-up to just the title bar
- A Raycast-style palette to search every list
- Free, for macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon
